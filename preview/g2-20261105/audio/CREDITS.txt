The clips are synthesized from the site's vocabulary and original English practice sentences.
Engine: Piper 2023.11.14-2
https://github.com/rhasspy/piper/releases/tag/2023.11.14-2

The V5 additions use British and American eSpeak-ng phonemes for the respective
models. New isolated clips use the word card's IPA. The example sentences use
contextual phonemes with targeted adjustments for the articulate and overestimate
endings. The new sentences are checked at 130–175 words per minute including pauses.
https://github.com/rhasspy/piper-phonemize/blob/master/src/phonemize.cpp

The G2 additions use the same models with fixed zero-noise settings. Isolated
terms use the word cards' IPA. Targeted sentence phonemes distinguish verb and
noun uses of affect, associate, attribute, and extract, and American noun stress
in address. Added example sentences are measured at 130–175 words per minute
including pauses.
